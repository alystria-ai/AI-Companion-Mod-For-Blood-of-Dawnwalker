# Dawnwalker Convai — v0.26.4

The mod adds summonable companions and Convai conversations to The Blood of Dawnwalker. F5 opens a 17-character roster: Anca, Lacra, Bakir, Ambrus, Xanthe, Brencis, Crake, Bakr-Erga, Leonica, Ocha, Vicho, Drogos, Sara, Catalin, Pieter, Vladimir and Isbrand. Summon multiple copies, select individual members in YOUR PARTY, or dismiss them individually. There is no configured party cap. Copies share their character’s Convai profile and conversation history while keeping separate movement and facial targets.

Companions follow and help in your fights automatically. Each character's native AI chooses attacks, positioning, timing and abilities. Short dodges and flanking near enemies keep them fighting. Sustained movement away from the fight asks them to break off after their current uninterruptible action and rejoin you. This is inferred from movement, enemy distance and time. They request Sprint promptly when you sprint or a gap opens, using native follower movement profiles. They use wider rows while travelling, with a direct-route fallback when a lane is obstructed. If they remain far behind in a fight, they regroup; safe off-camera catch-up accounts for the third-person camera and retries sooner when you outpace their sprint. F5 contains the roster, current party status and Help; Tactics and Orders have been removed. A sole Anca is simply Anca; two copies become Anca #1 and Anca #2, reverting to a plain name if one remains.

You can queue several summons without waiting between characters. The menu shows each request's loading stage and stays responsive during loading. It closes after the whole batch arrives, unless you select another character; F5 always closes it manually. A progress stage is not an estimate of remaining seconds. Native actor construction can still briefly stall the game itself.

Ask a companion to “follow me” or “stop walking” through conversation. Stop means wait here until you ask them to follow; closing chat does not cancel it. “Look at me” faces you during chat, and “leave” ends chat without dismissing a summoned companion. These actions are explained in Help, with no separate Actions tab.

Press F6 for a private text conversation or F7 to toggle the microphone. Face the body of a nearby NPC; precise face aiming is unnecessary. Enter or Send submits text, and Esc closes the composer. Replies have audio, compact timed subtitles and facial animation. Walk away to release the conversation. A summoned companion then resumes following unless you asked them to wait. Audio currently plays through the helper rather than a positional Unreal sound emitter.

F8 starts group text and F9 starts a group voice turn. The character you face answers first, or the nearest visible character if none is aimed at. Up to two other nearby companions respond in sequence. Known friends and family, faction connections, the subject and distance determine relevance. Three copies of one person do not become three speakers. Each reply uses the character’s own voice and facial target, and later speakers hear earlier public replies. The group microphone stops after your utterance; F9 starts another. A new conversation cancels the old round.

Memory combines sourced personal lore, 32 curated quest rules across the 17 companions, and things heard in group conversation. Every quest fact requires the correct identity, completed quest and, where needed, selected outcome. A character does not gain another person’s private discoveries merely because they share a faction. Heard statements retain their speaker and remain reports rather than verified history. Characters share memories between their summoned copies. Normal quest completion keeps history; detected save rewinds and altered completed outcomes use a separate memory timeline. Region, time, rain and snow are temporary context. Unknown quest branches remain unknown; the database is not an exhaustive walkthrough of every possible choice.

The installed helper starts automatically with UE4SS, maintains a Convai connection, and uses WebView2 rather than Electron. One active Convai client switches between named character sessions; no extra cloud character is made when you summon a copy. Voices are selected catalogue voices, not reproductions of the game actors. All named profiles use the verified `fast-gemma-4-31b-it` configuration. The helper handles network/audio; UE4SS Lua applies facial weights and coordinates native game AI. Code changes hot-reload, although reloads and world changes clear the spawned party.

The latest release has offline checks for companion state transitions, naming, pacing, native combat ownership, commands, chat and menu loading. The updated helper and UI compile and their layouts have been rendered. These checks do not prove the new combat/follow behavior in gameplay. Earlier gameplay confirmed the core facial shaping, Lacra's fighting/catch-up and Ambrus fighting on v0.26.1; the microphone was not physically tested. Loading may still hitch during native actor construction, and large parties cost game resources. Some boss abilities require player targets or campaign scripting and cannot be guaranteed against other NPCs. Summoning a defeated story character creates a companion copy and does not change the quest outcome.

For implementation and sources, see [README](README.md), [group/memory methodology](docs/GROUP-CHAT-AND-MEMORY.md) and [quest coverage](characters/QUEST-KNOWLEDGE.md).
