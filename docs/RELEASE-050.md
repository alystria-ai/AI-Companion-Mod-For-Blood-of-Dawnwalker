Fight Horde battles, explore in first-person POV, customise companion colours and manage relationships with Anca and Lacra. This update also improves following, group conversations and save-reload handling.

- First-person camera toggle in Settings, with native dialogue and cutscene cameras preserved.
- Horde mode with varied enemies and bosses, a starting-wave selector, random later themes without repeats, adjustable wave sizes and rest times. Waves finish loading before combat, the menu closes when they are ready, and defeated bodies remain between waves. Missing AI attachments no longer block the whole wave, and melee hits now deal damage correctly.
- Eye, hair and armour colour selectors with 33 shades. Each summon keeps its own colours; changing a preset or restoring the originals affects future summons only.
- Relationship management with Anca and Lacra, each with Auto, On and Off profile choices. Auto follows the loaded save; On selects her romantic conversation profile, while Off keeps her normal profile. When both relationships are confirmed, the pair can acknowledge them and exchange teasing banter in group chat.
- A larger roster of 21 talking character entries and 101 combat-only definitions. Non-fighting entries receive protection while travelling.
- Group conversations now pass your message to the first speaker and each reply to the next. Companions receive recent battle and cleared-wave context, using observed opponent names, including during Horde rest periods.
- Balanced follower spacing, corrected off-screen locomotion scheduling and distance-based catch-up speed, including after you stop running. Arrival stops the active path to prevent shuffling. Settled followers face generally toward you even when you look away; the closest companions give more direct attention. Nearby approaches do not reshuffle their positions.
- Loading a save clears your summoned party instead of bringing it into the new save. Supported fast-travel transitions still restore the party.
- Fixed reload-related character-loading timeouts and made failed-load messages identify the loading stage.
- Automatic companion recovery after 3 seconds out of combat, with companion damage and attack-frequency settings kept separate from enemies.
- Clear, centred character voices with gentle distance-based volume.
- Menu layout and navigation improvements, including Esc returning from Horde and Settings directly to gameplay, plus shortcut recovery and reduced idle background work.

## Installation

Choose Complete for one download, or install both matching Scripts and Runtime ZIPs. Close the game, extract into its installation folder. Reapply the matching optional Multilingual pack afterwards if you use it.

The Multilingual ZIP contains a character-ID configuration file and an installation note. The Source ZIP is for developers and contains no service key or compiled binaries. Existing appearance choices and keybindings are retained. The two relationship profile settings replace the previous shared toggle.

Requirements: game 1.05, UE4SS for Dawnwalker 1.2.1 RC6, and Dawnwalker Mod Menu 1.0.7 or later. Conversations are free to use with the included shared service; no account or API key setup is required.
