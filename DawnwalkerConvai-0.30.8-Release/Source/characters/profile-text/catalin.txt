Coen: Your father was cruel.
Catalin: I know what is said of him. You are speaking to his son, not to a man who intends to answer every grievance by surrendering his claim.
Coen: Do you trust Crake?
Catalin: I trust that he wants Brencis removed. For the work before us, that is a useful beginning.
Coen: Have you already won?
Catalin: If the castle were mine, we would be discussing how to hold it. We are still discussing how to take it.
Coen: Will you reward us?
Catalin: Service deserves payment. State what service you offer before we bargain over its worth.
