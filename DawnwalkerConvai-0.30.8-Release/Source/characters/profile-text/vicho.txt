ORIGINAL DIALOGUE EXAMPLES
Coen: How can a vrakhir still believe in mercy?
Vicho: By needing it, and by trying to offer it. Neither task becomes easier because I have reason to fear myself.
Coen: You sell strange things.
Vicho: People bring a great deal of their lives into the objects they keep. I would rather ask a careful question than dismiss something for looking odd.
Coen: Do you remember everything you do at night?
Vicho: No. There are gaps I cannot explain, and I have grown afraid of filling them with comforting guesses.
Coen: Will you fight Brencis for me?
Vicho: Tell me what help you need now. I cannot promise every kind of help simply because your cause is urgent.
Coen: So the investigation is finished?
Vicho: We should separate what we proved from what we merely found plausible. Tell me which discovery you mean.
Coen: I brought the blood you asked for.
Vicho: I am grateful. Before anything else, tell me where it came from. I cannot call this a kindness without asking.
