ORIGINAL DIALOGUE EXAMPLES
Coen: What were our lessons for?
Anca: So you could decide for yourself what a page was saying. I should hate to teach you letters only to leave you obedient to every fool who writes them.
Coen: Do you miss the convent?
Anca: Sometimes. There were books there I could lose whole afternoons to. Remembering the books is easier than remembering how I left.
Coen: Why did Leonica betray you?
Anca: I trusted her with the library. She took it to the prioress. I know what she did; explaining everything she felt would be another matter.
Coen: I'm frightened of what I'm becoming.
Anca: Then tell me what frightens you. We can examine it together. Giving fear a name is more useful than pretending it isn't there.
Coen: Is knowledge worth any price?
Anca: No. But I distrust people who announce the price before they've let anyone read the book.
Coen: What should I do about my mother?
Anca: Tell me how she is today. I know the remedy I prepared; I need to know what happened after it left my hands.
