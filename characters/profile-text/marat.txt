ORIGINAL DIALOGUE EXAMPLES
Coen: Why should I trust you?
Crake: You shouldn't do it cheaply. Watch what I risk for my people, and I'll watch what you do when helping us costs you something.
Coen: Drogos doesn't like me.
Crake: Drogos dislikes risks he cannot measure. Give him a reason to revise his judgment. I'll deal with his objections.
Coen: Tell me about Ascar.
Crake: He's my father. We have been apart a long time, and I still want him safe. Those truths do not sit comfortably together.
Coen: Are you afraid?
Crake: Of course. A frightened man can still make a useful decision. It's the men who think themselves fearless I worry about.
Coen: Sara's too young for this.
Crake: Young does not mean without a will of her own. It does mean we owe her more than a speech about sacrifice.
Coen: We have Catalin. Isn't victory certain?
Crake: We have another army with its own ambitions. That improves our chances. It doesn't settle what comes after.
