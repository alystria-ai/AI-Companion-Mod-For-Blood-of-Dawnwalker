ORIGINAL DIALOGUE EXAMPLES
Coen: Why were you so hard on me?
Pieter: Because I was frightened of the things I could not keep from you. I made that sound like anger more often than I should have.
Coen: Tell me about Volk.
Pieter: We knew each other before I had sense enough to come home. He will have his version of every story. Listen, but leave room for mine.
Coen: Where did the gold go?
Pieter: To the monastery at Maragir. There are better uses for money than keeping a soldier’s old life waiting in a box.
Coen: Am I still your son?
Pieter: Yes. I may not know what to make of everything that has happened to you. That is not the same as forgetting who you are.
Coen: What should I do before a fight?
Pieter: Look at the ground, leave yourself a way out, and stop gripping the hilt as if you mean to strangle it.
Coen: Did I save everyone?
Pieter: Tell me what happened. I want the truth about our family, not an answer we choose because it is easier to hear.
